# 🪵 Wooden Running Man Automation

A manufacturing engineering project involving the fabrication and automation of a wooden running-man mechanism using hands-on workshop manufacturing processes and available scrap materials.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Wooden Running Man Automation** project was completed as part of a Manufacturing Engineering module to develop practical knowledge and hands-on skills in different manufacturing technologies.

The project involved designing and fabricating a simple automated wooden running-man mechanism using materials and manufacturing techniques practiced during workshop sessions.

The product was fabricated as a group project, with emphasis placed on practical manufacturing, assembly, and the effective use of available materials. Scrap materials were utilized where possible to reduce material waste and demonstrate resourceful manufacturing practices.

The completed product was demonstrated at the end of the module, along with the fabrication process and the final automated operation.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Apply practical knowledge gained from manufacturing engineering workshop sessions.
* Fabricate a functional product using multiple manufacturing processes.
* Develop hands-on skills in manufacturing, fitting, assembly, and finishing.
* Make effective use of available scrap materials.
* Understand the sequence of operations involved in fabricating a functional product.
* Work collaboratively as a team to complete the manufacturing project.
* Demonstrate the completed product and its fabrication process.

---

## 🏭 Manufacturing Processes

The project provided practical experience in workshop-based manufacturing processes, including:

* **Wood Working**
* **Fitting**
* **Machining**
* **Assembly**

The manufacturing processes were selected and applied according to the requirements of the individual components and the overall construction of the running-man mechanism.

## 🛠️ Manufacturing Approach

The Wooden Running Man was fabricated through a sequence of practical workshop operations.

The required components were first planned and prepared based on the intended design. Available materials, including scrap materials where suitable, were utilized during fabrication.

Individual components were manufactured using appropriate workshop processes before being fitted and assembled to create the complete running-man mechanism.

After assembly, the mechanism was tested and adjusted to achieve the intended automated movement.
